Core Capabilities

Delivering data for critical infrastructure.

Bathymetry Survey

A bathymetric survey is the process of mapping the seafloor.

Geophysical Survey

Geophysical surveys are a vital tool for locating and mapping objects and features buried underground or underwater without the need for excavation.

Offshore Platform Site & Pipeline Route Surveys

Offshore platform and pipeline surveys are critical for the planning, design, and safe installation of subsea infrastructure.

Submarine Cable Route Surveys

Submarine cable route surveys are essential for the successful deployment and long-term operation of underwater communication and power cables.

Topographic Survey

A topographic survey is a detailed map of the physical features of a piece of land.

Gradiometer Surveys for UXO Search

Gradiometer surveys are a vital geophysical technique used to detect Unexploded Ordnance (UXO).

Shallow & Deep Water Oceanography

Shallow Water Oceanography, also known as coastal oceanography, focuses on the ocean environment from the shoreline to the edge of the continental shelf, typically to a depth of about 200 meters.

Weather Forecasting Services

Weather forecasting services use a combination of science, technology, and observation to predict the state of the atmosphere at a future time.

Wide Area Positioning Services

Wide Area Positioning Services (WAPS) are a type of augmentation system that provides improved accuracy for standard satellite navigation systems.

    Veripos LD900 DGNSS Equipment Training

    The Challenge

    Ocean Science & Surveying Pvt. Ltd. recently acquired the advanced Veripos LD900 DGNSS equipment to elevate our high-precision positioning capabilities. To ensure seamless offshore integration and optimal utilization, our field personnel required specialized, hands-on operational training.

    The Training (One Week)

    As an authorized service provider for Veripos, Ocean Science & Surveying Pvt. Ltd. recently partnered with Veripos Singapore Pte Ltd to conduct a comprehensive technical training program. Tailored specifically for our Engineers and Survey Engineers, the sessions covered complete system setup, software configuration, troubleshooting, and advanced operational techniques for the new LD900 DGNSS units.

    Impact & Outcome

    Our Engineering and Survey teams are now fully proficient in deploying and managing the Veripos LD900 DGNSS systems. This rigorous training ensures enhanced accuracy, maximum uptime, and reliable positioning data delivery for our clients across all upcoming offshore campaigns.

    Multibeam and Singlebeam Echosounder Training

    The Challenge

    Maintaining the highest standards of data acquisition requires our survey personnel to be thoroughly proficient with the latest multibeam and singlebeam echosounder technologies, calibration procedures, and troubleshooting.
